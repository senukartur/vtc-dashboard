# Trip reporting and review workspace

VTC-030 connects the protected RU/EN trip workflows to the existing server commands. Runtime activation remains separately controlled by the [trip](trips.md), [manual report](manual-reports.md), [decision](trip-decisions.md), [catalog](catalogs.md), [event](events.md) and [reconciliation](reconciliation.md) configuration. No production qualification thresholds, reporting timezone, storage capacity or hosted activation is supplied by this UI.

## Member workflows

- `/account/trips` offers own and accepted company scopes. It keeps scope, game, decision (own only), source, period, search and completion order in the URL. The decision filter names `rejected` "Needs fixes" ("Нужно исправить"), since such a trip can be fixed and resubmitted; trip badges keep "Rejected". A company link can specify a valid canonical driver ID in `ownerId`. The company scope uses its accepted-only server projection. Source filtering matches any canonical group with a source of that kind.
- The list defaults to All time and `completion_desc`. Calendar presets convert both dates independently to an inclusive UTC `from` and exclusive UTC `toExclusive` on `reportingAt`, using the viewer's saved display timezone. If the timezone is unavailable, the list keeps All time and links to preferences. Analytics periods use the company reporting timezone.
- Search trims and debounces up to 128 characters. It sends the active RU/EN locale and accepts a canonical trip number with or without a leading #. Effective filter changes reset the page; URL changes from controls also clear the selection. A rejected cursor restarts the same list at its first page.
- The result count is the API's authorized `total` before paging. The own lifetime fact line reads the unfiltered own trip status summary. The list displays assigned numbers and shows linked events and recorded game modes only in authorized own summaries. Displayed times use the saved display timezone and distinguish proven completion, staff confirmation and other reporting times. Catalog labels identify English fallback, and missing facts remain unavailable.
- At 1280px the search and filters share one row. At narrow widths the filter disclosure leaves the search visible, and at 800px and below trip rows become labeled cards with a completion order selector in the disclosure.
- From 1200px a plain row click or Enter selects a trip through a validated `?trip=<uuidv4>` parameter and moves focus to the panel heading. The selection stays in the browser, survives paging and can be restored by history. The non-modal panel reads `/trips/:tripId` for the authorized summary or private view. Close or Escape returns focus to the row or main content. While open, the panel shows the Sources column's content. Narrower screens link to the full detail page. The dispatch review queue has its own list with the same panel breakpoint.
- `/account/trips/:tripId` renders the server's summary/private discriminated union. Other members' accepted summaries never cause private evidence/history requests. Private detail includes normalized effective facts, original immutable manual revisions, comparison against the prior revision, decision reasons and historical evaluation references. Comparisons require a proven same-report predecessor with the preceding report-local revision number. Interleaved reports are never compared; legacy or missing lineage is explicitly unavailable. History pages remain bounded.
- The Drafts tab on `/account/trips?tab=drafts` and the existing `/account/reports` link use the owner's editing-only draft list. The tab count comes from `editingCount` before paging. Rows use localized city and cargo labels, and draft deletion requires a separate confirmation with the current draft ETag. A stale deletion refreshes the affected draft reads and requires a fresh confirmation. The editor preserves unsaved values and blocks writes after a version change or deletion conflict until the driver explicitly reloads the latest draft.
- `/account/reports/new`, `/account/reports/drafts/:draftId` and `/account/reports/:reportId/edit` save partial drafts or submit complete immutable revisions. Final writes require both reference times in order and at or before now. The form uses the account display timezone with a UTC fallback and sends millisecond UTC timestamps. A newly entered repeated minute uses its earlier instant. Nonexistent minutes are invalid. An unchanged saved minute keeps its exact instant, including sub-minute precision.
- The compact report editor groups each city with its optional company, then cargo and trip facts, evidence, and telemetry target. The depot selector pages visible same-game city depots and rechecks a selected company against a changed city before a write. Damage accepts zero and fractional values from 0 to 100. The form announces invalid entered reference values and passively lists missing requirements, including a ready screenshot. Save and delete are secondary to submission. The aside explains the review and revision workflow.
- The full private trip detail displays the current manual revision's reference times, damage and localized company names. Omitted historical values appear as Not specified. The company label reads are conditional on a company ID in that private manual revision. Reference facts do not change effective trip facts, reporting periods, qualification or progress.
- Upload controls read the configured policy and show processing, ready, rejected, expired and unavailable states. A ready completion screenshot is required before submission. Private downloads use the authorized binary route. Upload completion and removal update the latest attachment state, so a removed attachment cannot return when another upload settles. Files and object URLs are transient; report bodies, reasons and evidence are never persisted in browser storage. Retention remains at least one calendar year after the latest decision and throughout review.
- An explicit own same-game telemetry target carries its canonical version. Reloading targets is an explicit version refresh. A saved draft must successfully save a changed or refreshed target/version before submission can be confirmed, because the submission command uses the persisted draft target. Draft, report and canonical trip versions remain distinct. Accepted records offer a correction request, preserving the effective result until staff commits a replacement.
- Own trip detail exposes event association. Selection and RSVP do not prove attendance. The server either applies an authoritative result or creates a pending proposal; immutable event/attendance references are displayed during review.

## Staff workflows

VTC-136 selects the dispatch workspace from the single permission-filtered `/me/workspace-summary?view=stage3` cache used by navigation and the overview. Its `reviewQueue` selects Stage 3 and its `legacyReviewQueue` selects the legacy routes and commands described below. A loading, failed, or omitted review section has its own state. The shared attention count uses either authorized queue and preserves promotion counts independently.

The Stage 3 dispatch workspace opens Queue, Duplicates and Resolved at `/account/review`. Waiting count and oldest age come from `/trips/review-summary`, independent of filters. Queue uses the server's queue time, and Resolved uses the latest effective decision time in descending order. Game, source, reason code and canonical driver ID are URL filters. The driver picker reads a bounded page of member nicknames, and a filter change restarts paging while keeping the open panel. From 1200px the queue opens the side panel, and narrower screens open the full page with a filter-preserving back link. The server excludes the reviewer's own trips from lists and counts and denies direct access. The UI also withholds commands and private history from an own response.

The staged review compares the current report against the independent telemetry source snapshot, with exact localized quantities, timestamps, textual differences, and unavailable values distinct from zero. Direct acceptance may omit a note. Returning a trip requires a reason with inline feedback, and proposal decisions retain their required reason. The existing decision preconditions, over-limit confirmation, evidence, revisions and corrections remain in the workspace.

The Duplicates tab shows both trip summaries, supports game filtering, and uses the listed snapshot version for dismissals and undo. Dismissal requires a reason. The previous `/account/review/reconciliation` link opens Duplicates with its game filter and can retain the reconciliation history entry for an authorized trip. Waiting pairs can open the existing merge workflow, and applied operations retain their history and guarded reversal.

`/account/review` (VTC-059) is a compact dispatch queue with two tabs: Awaiting decision lists held trips (`hold=true`) oldest first by default, and Decided lists released trips newest first with a company-decision filter. Game, source (VTC-077) and order filters, the tab and the selected trip live in validated search parameters; filter changes restart paging and close the panel, and a cursor the server rejects (409 `VERSION_CONFLICT`, for example an expired one) returns to the first page of the same filters without an error. The source filter uses the trip list's vocabulary and labels and is sent as the `listTrips` `source` parameter with `scope=review` on both tabs; unknown values mean any source and are never sent. Like the game, it is kept across tab switches, cleared by Reset filters and carried by the full page's back link. A toolbar at least 1000px wide keeps the tabs and filters on one row; a narrower one (tablets, and desktops below about 1264px with the full sidebar, with or without the decision panel) shows the tabs with the timezone note and puts the filters on a full row below, on both tabs alike. The queue, the report's telemetry targets, recent company trips, driver trips and drafts send the interface language as `locale`, so labels follow it rather than the saved account language. The header's overall count is the workspace summary's, which leaves out the viewer's own trips; when the awaiting page lists any, the header says they are not counted because another reviewer decides them. Rows show driver (own reports marked), route, queue type (manual report, new revision, late telemetry or telemetry), revision number, a rules flag and the filing time; at 760px and below each row becomes a three-line card. From 1200px a row opens a non-modal decision panel beside the queue; narrower screens open `/account/review/:tripId`, whose back link keeps the queue filters. Both read the trip through `getTripReview` (trip, effective facts, qualification check, acceptance and decision history), the report's `listManualRevisions` page when a manual revision exists, catalog entity labels for pinned IDs, and evidence content only when a screenshot is opened; below the panel the full page also reads the private history (revisions, report, correction requests) and reconciliation history, and proposals once the corrections disclosure opens. The header names the driver, queue type and revision and states why the trip is held (its stored review reasons, localized where known and shown as the code otherwise). The panel then shows the revision diff against the proven same-report predecessor (changed fields marked in text) and the trip's effective facts (route, cargo, mass, trailers, driven and planned distance, start and completion; unknown values read as unavailable, never zero) whenever the read includes them, because telemetry or an approved staff resolution can change them without a new revision; trips with a telemetry source list the facts first, and a trip with neither facts nor a manual revision says its facts are unavailable. It continues with evidence opened on demand in a dialog, the qualification check (pass, fail, unknown or bypassed by an event), a merged revision/decision history with reviewer nicknames and the "Accepted over limits" marker, and the decision form. Staff choose Accept or Reject before the submit button is named; a reason is always required. An over-limit acceptance lists every exceeded limit and requires an explicit confirmation, which resets whenever the review ETag changes and is sent as `confirmOverLimits: true` only then; when the server reports acceptance as unavailable, Accept stays focusable and explains why while Reject remains. Own reports stay listed but show a note without decision controls. Escape closes the side panel and returns focus to the row, except on a decision control (choice, reason, confirmation, submit) or during text composition, so a draft is never discarded by Escape. Proposals and resolutions remain on the full page under a "Corrections and resolution" disclosure that opens while a proposal is pending. A decision binds the current review ETag and the exact immutable reviewed revision and fact. All manual authors in a merged group remain barred from their own review, resolution and reconciliation, including directors.

Direct decisions require an explanation. Resolution proposals contain only supported changed fields, ready evidence, optional exact pending correction request and optional historical assignment. Supported UTC corrections are offered only for telemetry facts; manual dates remain absent. Proposal decisions bind the exact proposal revision and current canonical ETag. The proposal-decision response union distinguishes a rejected proposal from a rejected effective trip.

`/account/review/reconciliation` shows bounded candidates, side-by-side authorized facts and explicit primary selection. Candidate reads retain current root/owner/game/matcher guards. When assessment alone changes a version, an advisory pair remains visible only after complete bounded owning evidence proves exactly the stored immutable source fact-ID set and each current effective pointer belongs to its own source set. Matching reasons and accepted-state warnings are recomputed; changed, incomplete or unprovable evidence is excluded. This read never updates candidates, searches, credits or decisions, and always returns `review_required`. It preserves the source reconciliation-before-assessment guard. The merge command still independently validates both current versions; a derived effective fact outside the original source set waits for renewed discovery rather than receiving this read-only exception. Confirmation carries both current versions. Merge retains evidence/sources, produces one canonical contribution and never adds the distances or independently accepts a trip. Trip detail contains reloadable applied/reversed reconciliation history. If a later canonical version invalidates a history cursor, Refresh restarts at its authoritative first page and clears the old page stack. Reversal reloads the operation and current original primary/secondary versions through the owning DTO; aliases still resolve to the canonical detail. Later decisions/dependencies can prevent reversal.

## Transport and recovery

Private query keys bind member, session generation, identity epoch, current capabilities, locale and effective path/filters/cursor. Cancellation reaches transport. Session loss clears private caches; permission failures remove private data and refresh current capabilities. HTTP failures are mapped without rendering private error bodies.

Writes carry current CSRF, actor-scoped UUID idempotency keys and exact ETags where required. Identical uncertain retries reuse their key; changed input, target or version receives a new key. Upload identity includes a SHA-256 checksum of the actual bytes, filename and MIME. Pending commands cannot duplicate. Retry-After is honored. A 409/428 preserves input, refreshes server state and closes the obsolete confirmation; the user must review the current state before confirming again. A background version change cannot rebind an open confirmation to a new command.

The frontend consumes generated browser types and schemas. The installed Zod JSON Schema importer lacks conditionals, so the two existing `ResolutionInput`/`TripProposal` conditions are expanded into equivalent exclusive object branches before import. Positive and negative contract fixtures cover omitted/null/exact correction links and non-null resolution changes.

## Verification

The normal contributor baseline is `pnpm contracts:check`, `pnpm check`, `pnpm build`, `pnpm test:local`, and the full `node deploy/development/test.mjs` rehearsal. Focused lanes are feedback only. The browser harness composes the built API's owning modules, independent synthetic keys and an allocation-owned private Linux evidence directory. It retains the normal five-slot pools and settles its worker before shutdown. Real trip browser journeys disable traces/video/screenshots; controlled synthetic RU/EN captures use ignored output at 390×844, 768×1024 and 1280×800.

Coverage is in `test/unit/trip-ui.test.ts`, `test/unit/manual-report-ui.test.ts` and `test/unit/review-ui.test.ts`, the existing real MySQL trip/decision/reconciliation suites, controlled `test/e2e/trips.spec.ts`, `test/e2e/trips-list.spec.ts`, `test/e2e/manual-report-layout.spec.ts` and `test/e2e/review-layout.spec.ts`, and real `test/e2e/trips-real.spec.ts`. Mocked success establishes UI behavior only. Exact-candidate independent verification/review, current-head advisory review, Required CI, merge and operational activation remain separate evidence gates.

Measured synthetic desktop upload feedback reduced one action from five GET reads plus one POST to two GET reads plus one POST: CSRF, upload and its new metadata. Unrelated events, upload policy and trip-list reads were removed. This is a request-count measurement on controlled fixture data, not throughput/capacity evidence. Paired live editing also exposed API pool admission failures; browser account/catalog/trip reads now share a cancellable FIFO scheduler with two active reads per tab, retaining each transport deadline. Queued cancellation never fetches, active cancellation/rejection releases capacity, and session/query cancellation cannot revive an obsolete queued read. It does not promise unlimited multi-client capacity or change server authority.
